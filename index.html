A playground for vibecoded projects. Static pages, no build step — everything here runs by opening a file.

Doodle Armature generative · canvas
Sheets of naive ink and pencil drawings. Every part is pinned to an invisible 3D solid and projected, so the whole thing turns. Eight drawing utensils, ordered-rank hatching, quality gates that reject and reroll bad specimens, and a 30fps animation driven off the armature itself.
