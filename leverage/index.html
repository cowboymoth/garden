How this is computed

Leverage Index (Tom Tango, used by FanGraphs) measures how much a moment can move things: the average swing you should expect from here, divided by the average swing of a typical plate appearance. 1.00 is a perfectly average moment. Each of the four things you can swing gets its own scale, so 1.00 always means average.

This at-bat is leverage over the rest of the plate appearance — at 0-0 that is exactly the classic Leverage Index. This pitch is the same idea for the single next pitch, measured against an average pitch.
