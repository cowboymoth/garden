How this is computed

Leverage Index (Tom Tango, used by FanGraphs) measures how much a moment can move the game: the average win-probability swing you should expect from here, divided by the average swing of a typical plate appearance. 1.00 is a perfectly average moment.

This at-bat is leverage over the rest of the plate appearance — at 0-0 that is exactly the classic Leverage Index. This pitch is the same idea for the single next pitch, normalised against an average pitch, so 1.00 is again average.
